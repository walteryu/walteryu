About & Philosophy

This site exists as a digital commonplace book—a personal, non-commercial notebook capturing notes at the intersection of logic systems, physical movement, and longevity planning.

Rather than managing time to achieve maximum productivity, the goals here lean toward structural life design: focusing on relationships, exploring wild mountain spaces, and building self-contained, offline AI experiments on personal hardware configurations.

Principles in Focus

  • Open Architecture: No tracking parameters, tracking pixels, or dynamic framework dependencies. Plain text designed to read easily on any device.
  • Active Vitality: Using mountain trails, high-altitude trekking, and physical endurance challenges as foundational structural points for extended health span.
